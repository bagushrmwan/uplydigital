UPLY DIGITAL V32.1 - EMAIL VIA WINDOWS STATIC IP

1. Deploy folder/ZIP V32.1 ke Vercel.
2. Vercel cukup memakai:
   EMAIL_ENABLED=true
   EMAIL_PROVIDER=windows
   BELIBAYAR_BACKEND_URL=https://uplyapi.duckdns.org
   BELIBAYAR_BACKEND_KEY=<sama dengan UPLY_BACKEND_KEY Windows>
3. Setelah Windows V32.1 aktif, hapus BREVO_API_KEY dari Vercel.
4. Credential Brevo disimpan hanya di C:\uply-backend\.env.
5. Cek /api/health dan pastikan email.runtimeConnected=true serta email.staticEgress=true.
